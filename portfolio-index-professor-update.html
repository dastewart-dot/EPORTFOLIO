ABOUT THE PRODUCER

Creative leadership shaped by experience.

I am a producer, writer, creator and actor with a background in leadership, business and psychology. My producing approach combines creative development with organization, communication and practical production planning. I am especially interested in character-driven drama, science fiction and stories about family, identity, ambition and second chances.

My graduate work at New York Film Academy strengthened my skills in development, line producing, production accounting, pitching, research, entertainment law, marketing, finance, collaboration, television development and entrepreneurship.

REFLECTIONS

How my producing practice developed.

1. Finding My Producer Voice

I learned that a producer also has a creative voice through the projects selected, questions asked, resources prioritized and the way a vision is communicated.

2. Collaboration and Communication

Production reinforced that every department needs information, expectations and a shared understanding of the creative goal.

3. From Idea to Production Package

Developing One Way Route taught me that a strong package demonstrates understanding of both the story and the work required to execute it.

4. Building a Television World

Full Circle taught me how character, theme, episode structure, a series bible and a pilot script work together to support continuing television storytelling.

5. Research as a Producing Tool

Research Methodologies taught me to use evidence to strengthen producing decisions instead of relying only on instinct.

6. Business Behind the Creative Work

Building Stew Creative Productions gave me a more complete view of producing as both a creative profession and a business.

7. Curation and Professional Presentation

Revising this portfolio helped me focus on what each artifact demonstrates about my abilities as a producer.

8. Moving Forward

My next step is to keep developing original work, strengthen Stew Creative Productions and continue building professional collaborations.
